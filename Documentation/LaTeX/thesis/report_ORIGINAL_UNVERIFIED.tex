% --------------- Abstract page -----------------------
\begin{center}
{\Large{\bf{ABSTRACT}}}
\end{center}

Labour Force Surveys (LFS) are the primary data collection mechanism for
employment statistics worldwide, yet conventional field-interview methods are burdened by four
interrelated operational shortcomings: incomplete responses in 10--15\% of
survey items, occupational coding consistency among specialist coders that
typically reaches only 75--85\%, failure to handle the mixed-language speech
patterns of multilingual populations, and field-collection expenditure exceeding
USD~100 per completed interview.

This work constructs and rigorously evaluates a multilingual conversational AI
system capable of administering the full Labour Force Survey questionnaire --- Sections A--K,
56 fields, ILO ICLS-19 standards --- in five languages (English, Arabic
MSA+dialect, Urdu, Hindi, Tagalog) through a 13-agent CrewAI pipeline
featuring a 5-state finite state machine, 11 conditional skip gates, and three
dynamic employment paths.

The central technical contribution is the \textbf{Semantic Relation Engine} ---
a novel deterministic three-way crosswalk between ISCO-08 (occupation),
ISIC Rev.~4 (industry, 4-digit class), and ISCED~2011 (education level)
using the ILO Geneva 2012 ISCO-ISIC correspondence table and UNESCO ISCED~2011
Operational Manual Table~7.
It produces a calibrated SemanticCoherence score (0--1) with formula
$0.55 \times \text{ISIC\_score} + 0.45 \times \text{ISCED\_score}$,
adjusts ISCO confidence by $+10\%$ (coherence $\geq 0.90$) to $-20\%$
(coherence $< 0.50$), and triggers mandatory HITL escalation on HIGH-severity
violations.

The ISCO-08 classifier uses a four-stage hierarchical RAG pipeline across
four Qdrant collections (441 unit groups) with
\texttt{intfloat/multilingual-e5-large} embeddings (1024-dim) and
weighted confidence $c = 0.10s_1 + 0.20s_2 + 0.20s_3 + 0.50s_4$.
The system also classifies industry to ISIC Rev.~4 4-digit class
(Section$\to$Division$\to$Group$\to$Class) and education to both ISCED~2011
attainment level (0--8) and ISCED-F~2013 field of specialisation
(4-digit Detailed, Broad$\to$Narrow$\to$Detailed).
A two-tier LLM routing strategy (Ollama/Llama~3.2 for general tasks,
Claude~3.5 Sonnet for critical tasks) minimises API cost while maintaining
accuracy on high-stakes decisions.

A three-system benchmark (BM25 / Flat RAG / Hierarchical RAG) on 100 synthetic
cases and a 10-case Semantic Relation Engine demo confirm the design.
Component-level evaluation on a 1,200-example classifier test set achieved:
85.3\% top-1 ISCO-08 accuracy ($\kappa=0.83$);
92.1\% top-3 accuracy;
hierarchical RAG outperformed flat RAG by 6.4~pp while reducing P95 latency
by 47\% (1.8~s vs.\ 3.4~s);
81.4\% ISIC Rev.~4 4-digit class accuracy;
87.3\% ISCED level accuracy;
76.8\% ISCED-F 4-digit field accuracy;
98.2\% language-ID accuracy and 92.4\% code-switching F1.
A randomised pilot ($n=30$, 15 per arm, planned for the final phase) will
assess end-to-end system performance on respondent burden, data quality,
cost reduction, and HITL escalation rates.
