\section{Summary}
\label{sec:conc-summary}
% ------------------------------------------------------------

The research documented here designed and constructed a working prototype of
a multilingual conversational AI system for administering the full Labour
Force Survey questionnaire
(Sections A--K, 56 fields, ILO ICLS-19 standards) in five languages through a
13-agent CrewAI pipeline.
The central contribution --- the \textbf{Semantic Relation Engine} --- provides
the first deterministic three-way coherence validation between ISCO-08, ISIC
Rev.~4, and ISCED~2011 classifications using official ILO/UNESCO correspondence
tables, producing a calibrated SemanticCoherence score that adjusts confidence
and triggers HITL escalation on HIGH-severity violations.

A four-stage hierarchical ISCO-08 RAG pipeline across four Qdrant collections
(441 unit groups) outperformed flat RAG by 6.4 percentage points top-1 accuracy
(85.3\% vs 78.9\%) while reducing P95 latency by 47\% (1.8s vs 3.4s).
The system simultaneously classified industry to ISIC Rev.~4 4-digit class
(81.4\% top-1) and education to ISCED-F~2013 4-digit field (76.8\%) --- the
first conversational survey system to achieve triple-standard classification.

The prototype benchmark evaluation on 100 synthetic cases and component-level
testing established the following baseline performance indicators:
85.3\% top-1 ISCO-08 accuracy, 92.1\% top-3 accuracy, 98.2\% language
detection accuracy, and 1.8~s P95 RAG latency.
Full pilot study results ($n=30$, randomised controlled design) will be
reported in the final phase of the thesis, approximately 6 months from
the current pre-final submission.

\section{Contributions}
\label{sec:conc-contributions}
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\noindent\textbf{C1 --- Semantic Relation Engine.}
First deterministic three-way ISCO$\leftrightarrow$ISIC$\leftrightarrow$ISCED
coherence engine using ILO/UNESCO tables; SemanticCoherence score; four
severity levels; sub-major-group strict rules; bilingual EN+AR explanations;
integrated into every API response.

\noindent\textbf{C2 --- Complete LFS in 5 languages.}
Full 56-field Sections A--K (ILO ICLS-19), 3 employment paths, 11 skip gates,
E1 wage gate, F3 re-routing rule, in EN/AR-MSA/AR-dialect/UR/HI/TL.

\noindent\textbf{C3 --- Four-stage hierarchical ISCO-08 RAG.}
441 unit groups; 4 Qdrant collections; parent-code payload filtering; synonym
enrichment; keyword pre-filter; weighted confidence
$c = 0.10s_1 + 0.20s_2 + 0.20s_3 + 0.50s_4$; LLM re-ranking skip at
$\geq$0.92.

\noindent\textbf{C4 --- Triple-standard simultaneous classification.}
ISCO-08 (4-digit) + ISIC Rev.~4 (4-digit class) + ISCED~2011 (level 0--8) +
ISCED-F~2013 (4-digit detailed field) in a single conversational session.

\noindent\textbf{C5 --- Production open-source deployment.}
Dockerised 5-service stack; 11-table PostgreSQL schema; Email OTP + JWT;
GDPR Art.~15 compliant audit logging; bilingual report; supervisor HITL
dashboard; full pytest suite (18 files, fully mocked).

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:conc-limitations}
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\noindent\textbf{L1 --- Student pilot population.}
College students aged 18--25 are more digitally literate than the general LFS
population; results may not fully generalise.

\noindent\textbf{L2 --- ISIC Rev.~4 partial coverage.}
100+ of 238 four-digit class entries are covered; uncommon industries are less
accurately classified.

\noindent\textbf{L3 --- Simulated person register.}
Person Register pre-fill uses previous-round survey data, not a live
government population register.

\noindent\textbf{L4 --- Tagalog NER limited.}
NER for Tagalog relies primarily on the general Llama~3.2 model without
Tagalog-specific fine-tuning; entity extraction quality is lower than for
Arabic and English.

\noindent\textbf{L5 --- Underpowered secondary outcomes.}
$n=30$ is underpowered for secondary outcomes such as abandonment rate ($n>100$
needed).

\noindent\textbf{L6 --- Commercial LLM dependency.}
Claude~3.5 Sonnet is required for critical tasks; NSOs requiring fully sovereign
deployment need open-weight LLM benchmarking (see F1 below).

\noindent\textbf{L7 --- ISCED-F field coverage.}
The ISCED-F~2013 classifier currently covers 30+ of the 280+ detailed four-digit
fields defined in the UNESCO operational manual. Rare fields of specialisation
may be misclassified or fall back to the Narrow (two-digit) level.

\section{Future Research Directions}
\label{sec:conc-future}
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\noindent\textbf{F1 --- Open-weight LLM benchmarking.}
Benchmark Llama~3.1 70B and Qwen~2.5 on the ISCO re-ranking and bilingual
report tasks to enable fully air-gapped NSO deployment without commercial API
dependency.

\noindent\textbf{F2 --- Live national person register integration.}
Replace the simulated person register with live access to the national
Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security (ICP) registry
under appropriate data-governance agreements, enabling real 40--50\% question
reduction.

\noindent\textbf{F3 --- Full ISIC Rev.~4 coverage.}
Expand the ISIC knowledge base from 100+ to all 238 four-digit class entries
through corpus collection from NSO industry registers and ILOSTAT establishment
survey data.

\noindent\textbf{F4 --- ISCO revision readiness.}
When the ILO publishes an updated ISCO revision, evaluate the parallel-reindex
protocol for migrating all four Qdrant collections without downtime.

\noindent\textbf{F5 --- Fairness and equity auditing.}
Conduct a disaggregated analysis of ISCO-08 accuracy across demographic
subgroups (nationality, education level, informal vs.\ formal employment) to
identify and mitigate systematic classification biases before large-scale NSO
deployment.

\noindent\textbf{F6 --- Representative population validation.}
Conduct a subsequent NSO-sponsored study with respondents drawn from the
Labour Force Survey sampling frame, including older workers, informal-sector
employees, and populations with lower digital literacy.

\noindent\textbf{F7 --- Generalisation to other official surveys.}
Apply the hierarchical RAG + Semantic Relation Engine pattern to other official
survey instruments: Household Income and Expenditure Survey, Health Survey,
Establishment Survey, and the national Census questionnaire.
