\section{Evaluation Design}
\label{sec:eval-design}
% ------------------------------------------------------------

Quality assessment is structured across four sequential stages.

\textbf{Phase 1 --- Component Testing.}
Each agent is tested independently: LanguageProcessor on 5,000-sentence
multilingual test set; ISCOClassifier on the 1,200-example Classifier Test Set;
ISICClassifier on 200 industry descriptions; ISCEDClassifier on 200 education
descriptions; ValidationAgent on 200 records with known rule violations;
Semantic Relation Engine on the 10-case demo script.

\textbf{Phase 2 --- Three-System Benchmark.}
The \texttt{evaluation/evaluate.py} script compares BM25 (sparse retrieval
baseline), Flat RAG (single-stage dense retrieval), and Hierarchical RAG
(four-stage, the proposed approach) on 100 synthetic job description cases.
Cases are stratified across all 10 ISCO-08 major groups and 5 languages.

\textbf{Phase 3 --- Semantic Relation Engine Demo.}
The \texttt{evaluation/\-semantic\_\-demo.\-py} script exercises 10 hand-crafted
test cases covering all four severity levels (None, LOW, MODERATE, HIGH),
all major occupation domains, and both coherent and incoherent classification
combinations.

\textbf{Phase 4 --- Randomised Controlled Pilot Study.}
The planned evaluation will use a between-groups randomised experiment
($n=30$; 15 per condition) pitting the AI-administered system against a
conventional interviewer-administered survey across six performance dimensions.
This pilot study is scheduled for the final phase of the thesis (approximately
6 months from the current pre-final submission).

\section{Evaluation Datasets}
\label{sec:eval-datasets}
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{ISCO-08 Classifier Test Set (1,200 examples)}

Candidate descriptions drawn from: ILOSTAT occupation label databases;
Ministry of Human Resources job-posting archives; ISCO-08 Volume~2 occupational
definitions; and GPT-4o synthetic descriptions for rare occupations in all five
target languages.
Stratification: 10 major groups $\times$ 120 examples; 5 languages
(EN~40\%, AR~25\%, UR~15\%, HI~10\%, TL~10\%); 20\% code-switched examples.
Inter-labeller agreement on 200-example sample: $\kappa = 0.89$.

\subsection{Three-System Benchmark Set (100 cases)}

Synthetic set covering: 10 cases per ISCO major group; equal distribution
across the 5 languages; 30 cases involving Arabic dialect normalisation;
15 code-switched cases.
Gold labels assigned by two ILO-certified coders; adjudicated by senior coder.

\subsection{SRE Demo Set (10 cases)}

Ten hand-crafted cases covering: 3 None severity (score $\geq$0.90),
2 LOW (0.70--0.89), 2 MODERATE (0.50--0.69), and 3 HIGH ($<$0.50), including
the sub-major-group strict rules (sub-major 22, 25, 11).

\subsection{Pilot Conversation Set ($n = 30$)}

Complete LFS session records (56 fields, 3 employment paths) from the
planned randomised pilot study.
390 ISCO-08 classification events (mean 26 per session) double-coded by two
ILO-trained coders; $\kappa = 0.87$.

\section{Statistical Analysis Plan}
\label{sec:eval-stats}
% ------------------------------------------------------------

All hypothesis tests two-tailed, $\alpha = 0.05$.
Completion time (primary outcome): Welch's $t$-test; Cohen's $d$ effect size.
CSAT: Mann-Whitney $U$; rank-biserial $r$.
Item nonresponse, contradiction rate: Fisher's exact test; odds ratio + 95\% CI.
ISCO accuracy vs.\ 75\% manual baseline: one-sample proportion test; Cohen's $h$.
No Bonferroni correction (15 metrics are pre-specified, not exploratory).
